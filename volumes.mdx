---
title: "Volumes"
---

Volumes store datasets, model weights, and outputs that need to persist beyond a single run. Their contents can be accessed directly through the SDK or mounted as files inside a function.

## Creating a volume

`client.create_volume()` creates a volume and returns a `VolumeHandle`. OuterProduct manages the underlying storage by default.
